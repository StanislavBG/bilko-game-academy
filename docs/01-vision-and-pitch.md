# Vision & Pitch

## What we're building

**Bilko Game Academy** is a self-owned, ad-free, IAP-free Progressive Web
App that hosts a growing library of 2D games. Installable to iPad, desktop
web, and PC from a single codebase. Every game is independent in progression
and save; every game shares the platform's settings, controls, audio,
accessibility, and (optionally) cloud-save identity.

## Why

- **For the user:** one place, one install, one identity — play any of the
  house games with settings you set once.
- **For us:** a durable platform to ship games over years as a hobby/family
  project, without fighting app stores, ad SDKs, or IAP plumbing.

## What's in it at launch

1. The shell: home, game library, profile, leaderboards, settings.
2. **Boat Shooter** — the first game. A top-down vertical-scrolling arcade
   shmup × *Vampire Survivors*-style weapon stacking, set in the Age of
   Sail with cursed supernatural and volcanic acts.

## Non-goals at launch

- No ads, no IAP, no account required for local play.
- No app-store distribution in v1 (PWA only).
- No multiplayer (solo only; async leaderboards provide social).
- No voice acting.
