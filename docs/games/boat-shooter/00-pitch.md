# Boat Shooter — One-Page Pitch

**Top-down vertical-scrolling arcade shmup × *Vampire Survivors*-style weapon
stacking, in the Age of Sail.**

You pilot a single hero ship **down a river** — the river scrolls past beneath
you as you travel "downstream" (you move up the screen). Enemies attack
from all sides: ramming pirate skiffs, thundering navy gunboats,
ghostly galleons, volcanic warlords. You pick up weapons mid-fight; they
all fire at once. Stack six of them, stack six passives, reach the boss.

Every campaign run is a **30–50 minute roguelite ride** through 15 stages
across three acts — **the Sunlit Delta, the Cursed Fog, the Volcanic
Reach** — culminating in a screen-filling fight with the Kraken Ancient.
Weapons carry over between stages; die once, start over. Permanent gem
upgrades and cosmetics make every attempt incrementally better.

## Core hook

**Bullet-hell satisfying screen chaos meets "one more build" combinatorics.**
Six weapons firing simultaneously, 13 elemental reactions (Electrocute,
Shatter, Supernova), eight dramatic evolutions that transform your weapons
into gold-tier forms, and a bestiary of 18 enemy archetypes plus 9 bosses
each with a unique mechanic.

## Audience

Family-friendly (ESRB E / PEGI 7). Cartoon combat — cannons fire smoke &
sparks, enemies sink with splashes, no blood. Supernatural act is
spooky-not-horror; volcanic act is dramatic-not-grisly.

## Scope (v1)

- **13 weapons**, **8 passives**, **8 evolutions**, **5 L5 masteries**.
- **18 enemies** (15 regular + 3 mini-bosses) + **6 full bosses**.
- **15 stages** + **5 hidden stages** via treasure maps.
- **4 leaderboards**: campaign speedrun, per-stage, boss times, weekly challenge.
- **7 meta-progression tracks** with exponential gem curve.
- **Solo only**; async social via leaderboards.
- **Free, no ads, no IAP.**

## Playing for a minute

Drag your finger on the iPad to steer. Cannons fire automatically. Pick up
a Flamethrower — now you fire cannons *and* breathe fire. Pick up a Chain
Lightning — now cannons, fire, *and* lightning. Stack a Storm Compass
passive; chain-lightning gets bigger. Level up 3 times; pick a Harpoon;
now you spear-pull small enemies toward you. A Ghost Ship appears in the
fog; you're Wet from drifting through Kraken Ink; the lightning chains hit
the ghost while it's Wet → **Electrocute!** — ×4 damage explosion.
Boss drops a Shipwright Chest; Powder Barrel + Bow Cannon at L5 → your
Bow Cannon evolves into **Cannonade Supreme** (6 projectiles in a spiral
plus a periodic heavy mortar shell). Continue to stage 2.
