# Evolutions — 8 gold-tier weapons + 5 L5 masteries

## Trigger rules

1. Weapon at **L5** (max level).
2. Paired **passive held** (any level).
3. Open a **Shipwright Chest** (boss or mini-boss drop; final boss gives
   a wild-card chest that ignores the pair requirement).

Evolved weapons **replace** the original in the slot (not stacked). Dramatic
transform — new visual, new behavior, new VFX, unique audio cue.
**Evolutions cannot level further.**

## E1. Cannonade Supreme
- 6 projectiles in a rotating spiral, 3 dmg, pierce 3, 4 shots/s.
- Every 6th shot is a HEAVY MORTAR-SHELL arcing to nearest enemy: 10 dmg, 120 px AoE.
- Bronze cannon skin, gold muzzle-flashes, spiraling trail particles; heavy shell whistles inbound.

## E2. Thunderclap Broadside
- All 10 cannons fire simultaneously every 1.2 s in a synchronized BOOM.
- Each volley releases a radial SHOCKWAVE dealing 3 dmg + knockback within 300 px.
- Projectiles: 5/side, 4 dmg, pierce 2.
- Screen-shake on each beat; golden shockwave ring expands outward.

## E3. Tempest Cannonade
- Permanent storm cloud trails 200 px above the boat.
- Every 0.4 s a lightning bolt strikes a random enemy below, chaining to UNLIMITED nearby within 200 px.
- 4 dmg/target, no falloff, 0.3 s stun.
- Churning cloud, screen-illuminating lightning, thunder, rain.

## E4. Inferno Breath
- 360° fire aura, 320 px radius, 3 dmg/tick @ 8 ticks/s.
- Applies Burn 6 dmg/s for 6 s on entry.
- Burning enemies that die EXPLODE (60 px, 4 dmg).
- Dead enemies in fire leave a 2 s oil slick.
- Wall of flame rolling off the boat, red screen tint.

## E5. Sawblade Fortress
- TWO concentric orbit rings:
  - Inner: 6 fast axes @ 180°/s, 100 px radius, 3 dmg, pierce 2.
  - Outer: 10 slow blades @ 90°/s, 180 px radius, 2 dmg, pierce 2.
- Any enemy hitting the boat reflects 5 dmg + knockback.
- Combined with L5 Copper Hull iframes → very hard to damage.

## E6. Leviathan Ink
- Ink cloud expands to 350 px, follows the boat.
- Inside: 4 dmg/tick @ 6 ticks/s, -60% slow, Poison 3 dmg/s for 6 s post-exit.
- Every 3 s a GIANT TENTACLE emerges from within the ink and slams the highest-HP enemy in range for 25 dmg + 1 s stun.
- **Ink poison BYPASSES boss immunity.**
- Deep purple ink swirls, cyan glowing eye, tentacles from water.

## E7. Minefield
- Boat drops 4 mines/sec in a fan behind + sides.
- Mines persist until detonation (no 8 s timer).
- Weak homing within 150 px.
- Each enemy killed by a mine spawns 2 new mines at the death spot.
- Per-mine: 5 dmg, 80 px blast, chain-detonate within 120 px.

## E8. Ghost Armada
- 6 ghost-crew permanently ring the boat at 120 px orbit.
- Each ghost auto-fires 2 shots/s, 2 dmg, +100% vs Supernatural.
- If the boat is hit, one ghost sacrifices itself to save HP (2 dmg → 0, costs 1 ghost).
- Ghosts respawn 1 at a time every 15 s in combat.
