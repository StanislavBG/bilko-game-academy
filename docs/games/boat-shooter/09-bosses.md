# Full Bosses — 6 total

Mini-bosses are specced in `08-enemies.md` (Frigate Captain, Pirate Champion, Banshee Galleon).

Each full boss targets **~90–120 s** (final boss ~180 s) and has **a unique
mechanic no other boss uses**.

## B1. The Delta Commodore — "HMS Ironclad Majesty" (Stage 3)

**Unique: FLEET FORMATION.** 3 escort gunboats in V formation; while ≥1
escort alive, Commodore takes **-50% damage**. Shielded until escorts die.

- HP 200 (P1 80, P2 80, P3 40). Armor 4. Speed 90 px/s. Contact 5 dmg. Knockback-immune. Target ~110 s.
- P1 "The Fleet" (200 → 120): 3 escorts HP 20/Armor 2 in V formation; Commodore broadsides every 3 s (6 cannons/side, 2 dmg); escorts fire triple-cannon volleys every 4 s. -50% Commodore dmg while any escort lives.
- P2 "Broken Formation" (120 → 40): escorts dead, Commodore alone; alternating broadsides every 2 s; summons 2 Patrol Gunboats every 10 s; mortar barrage every 7 s (1 s telegraph, 80 px AoE, 3 dmg).
- P3 "Last Colors" (40 → 0): enrage (1.2 s broadside cadence); final distress flare summons one wave of 5 Scout Skiffs; early defeat cinematic at HP 5.
- **Drops:** 120 coins, 6 gems, 2 map fragments, Shipwright Chest, "Commodore's Ledger" (paper wake particles).
- **Flavor:** "Prepare to be boarded." Defeat: ironclad catches fire, tilts, sinks with ceremonial salute.

## B2. Pirate King "Admiral Scurvy" — Crimson Maw (Stage 5, Act I finale)

**Unique: BOARDING EVENT.** During P2, main fight pauses; camera zooms to
boat deck; 6 pirate boarders (HP 3 each) appear on deck; player has 10 s
to clear or takes 2 dmg per unkilled boarder. Happens 2× in P2.

- HP 220 (P1 100, P2 80, P3 40). Armor 4. Speed 100 px/s. Contact 5 dmg. Knockback-immune. Target ~115 s.
- P1 "Red Sails" (220 → 120): triple broadside every 3.5 s (5 cannons/side, 2 dmg); 2 Grappling Boarders every 8 s; chain-shot pair every 12 s (pierces 3, 3 dmg each).
- P2 "Boarding Deck" (120 → 40): Boarding Events at HP 100 and HP 60. Between events: cannon 2.5 s, 2 Ramming Brigands every 10 s; chain-shot every 6 s.
- P3 "Death Before Dishonor" (40 → 0): Scurvy on deck with blunderbuss + cutlass; enrage (+50% fire rate) if any boarder is attached; blunderbuss every 3 s (4 projectiles wide spread, 3 dmg each); final charge at HP 10.
- **Drops:** 150 coins, 8 gems, 2 map fragments, Shipwright Chest, "Scurvy's Hat" (tricorn + parrot pet).
- **Flavor:** "HAR HAR! Bring me their flag!" Defeat: Scurvy leaps overboard cursing, parrot flies off.

## B3. The Ghost Commodore — HMS Regret (Stage 8)

**Unique: POSSESSION.** Every 20 s (P2+), attempts to possess the boat.
Telegraph: swirling green aura over 1.5 s. If player stays in radius full
1.5 s → POSSESSED 3 s: controls inverted, all weapons muted, screen green-
tinted. Break line-of-sight / hide behind terrain to avoid.

- HP 250 (P1 100, P2 100, P3 50). Armor 5 physical / 2 lightning. Speed 85 px/s. Contact 4 dmg (phase-ignores iframes). Target ~120 s.
- **Immunities:** Water/Ink 100%, Fear.
- **Vulnerabilities:** ×2 lightning; ×1.5 Ghost Armada; Wet+Shock reaction ×6 damage.
- P1 "The Hallowed Deck" (250 → 150): 8-cannon spectral volley every 3 s (passes walls); wail every 10 s (120 px AoE, 2 dmg, Fear 1 s); 1 Ghost Ship every 18 s. **No possession yet.**
- P2 "The Binding" (150 → 50): possession attempts every 20 s; wail every 7 s; cannon every 2 s; if possession lands, ironclad fires heavy mortar (5 dmg, 100 px AoE) during possession.
- P3 "Soul Binding" (50 → 0): ironclad breaks apart; spirit free-roams (20×20 target, 180 px/s); continuous spectral cannon every 0.8 s (2 dmg); possession cadence 10 s / duration 2 s; Cursed Swarm waves of 10 every 20 s.
- **Drops:** 180 coins, 10 gems, 3 map fragments, Shipwright Chest, "Ghost Commodore's Cape" (translucent sails).
- **Flavor:** fog thickens; translucent officers emerge. Defeat: spirit dissolves; ironclad sinks.

## B4. The Drowned Admiralty (Stage 10, Act II finale)

**Unique: TRIPLE TARGET.** 3 cursed ships simultaneously; Soul Flagship
shields the other two (intangible while Flagship lives). Kill order is
strategic.

- HP 360 across 3 ships:
  - Soul Flagship (HP 160, Armor 4) — cannons + shield aura.
  - Soul Interceptor (HP 100, Armor 3) — ram + grapple (intangible in P1).
  - Soul Wraith (HP 100, Armor 5) — wails + summons (intangible in P1).
- Target ~140 s.
- P1 "Colors Of The Damned" (all 3 alive): Flagship 8-cannon spectral broadside every 2.5 s (ignores walls, 2 dmg each); blue shield aura; Interceptor wanders randomly; Wraith wails softly (1 dmg/s in 200 px). Must focus Flagship.
- P2 "Unleashed" (Flagship dead): Interceptor aggressive — 300 px/s ramming + grapple every 8 s (150 px pull); Wraith aggressive — wail every 5 s (300 px AoE, 3 dmg, Fear 1 s); both vulnerable. Player picks kill order.
- P3 "Requiem" (1 ship left): last-alive enters Requiem. Interceptor Requiem: continuous 220 px/s ram + 3 mirror decoys. Wraith Requiem: wail every 3 s + Cursed Swarm waves of 30 every 12 s + Kraken Tentacle. Kill → gem-shower of 8 gems.
- **Drops:** 250 coins, 15 gems, 4 map fragments, Shipwright Chest, "Drowned Admiralty Banner" (ghostly stern banner).
- **Flavor:** 3 ships emerge chained by glowing soul-chains. Defeat: chains break, ships sink in sequence, soul-wisps drift into fog.

## B5. The Obsidian Warlord — Captain Magmar (Stage 13)

**Unique: ARENA HAZARDS.** The volcanic stage itself participates — lava
geysers, lava waves, volcanic bombs — coordinated with boss phases.

- HP 300 (P1 120, P2 120, P3 60). Armor 6. Speed 100 px/s. Contact 6 dmg. Knockback-immune. Target ~130 s.
- **Immunities:** Burn 100%; fire weapons (Flamethrower, Inferno Breath, Fire-Arrow Rain) do **0 damage**.
- **Vulnerabilities:** ×3 Water/Ink; Freeze reaction ×4; Wet+Shock auto-triggers Cataclysm reaction.
- P1 "Eruption" (300 → 180): 3 permanent lava-geyser hotspots cycle (3 dmg/tick in spot); Warlord broadsides every 3 s (4 cannons, 3 dmg); Fire Patch every 8 s (100 px, 2 dmg/tick, 10 s duration); volcanic bomb shower every 12 s (3 bombs, 80 px AoE, 4 dmg, 1 s telegraph).
- P2 "The Wave" (180 → 60): Lava Wave rolls vertically every 15 s (stay ahead or 8 dmg); cannon every 2 s; Fire patches every 5 s; 2 burning-trail Ramming Brigands every 10 s.
- P3 "Volcanic Rage" (60 → 0): ship erupts — 6 continuous lava fountains (40 px, 2 dmg/tick); ship rams with fire-trail wake; Lava waves every 10 s; Fire patches every 2 s. At HP 20: volcano erupts, ash darkens screen to 50% visibility.
- **Drops:** 280 coins, 18 gems, 5 map fragments, Shipwright Chest, "Obsidian Crown" (helmet + glowing orange hull veins).
- **Flavor:** volcano erupts; ship rises through lava. Defeat: shatters into obsidian shards; volcano crumbles.

## B6. The Kraken Ancient (Stage 15, FINAL BOSS)

**Unique: SCREEN-SIZED BOSS.** Body fills lower half of screen; only
tentacles / head / core are targetable; destroyed parts **regrow in 8 s**.
4-phase structure.

- HP 500 distributed:
  - Tentacles: 20 HP each (up to 4 simultaneous + regrow).
  - Head: 200 HP (P2 only).
  - Core: 100 HP (P3, intermittently visible).
- Armor: 4 tentacles, 6 head, 2 core. Contact 6 dmg (tentacles on sweep). Target ~180 s.
- **Immunities:** Poison 100%, Fear 100%, Freeze immune (too big).
- **Vulnerabilities:** Electrocute ×2; Supernova 3-way reaction does 100 direct damage to core.
- **P1 "The Gathering"** (4 tentacles, 80 HP): 4 tentacles always active; kill all 4 to advance; destroyed regrow in 8 s; each tentacle has its own attack (sweep, grab, venom spit, lightning-conductor). Body submerged (cannot damage directly).
- **P2 "The Rising"** (Head HP 200): Kraken rises; body visible fills half screen; HEAD is target; 2 tentacles continue (respawn 12 s); head breathes ink (100 px aura, 2 dmg/tick + Wet); fires water-jet beams every 5 s (500 px, 4 dmg); Cursed Swarm waves every 18 s.
- **P3 "The Heart"** (Core HP 100): head mouth breaks open; CORE visible with iframe cycle (vulnerable 2 s / iframe 3 s, color-telegraphed); 3 tentacles go berserk (up to 5 simultaneous); head continues jets + ink; screen slowly dims from ink.
- **P4 "The Fall"** (Core ≤ 20): Kraken rages; everything maxes; continuous tentacles, jets, screen-flashes. At HP 0: cinematic — Kraken sinks, river recedes, title card **"THE RIVER IS YOURS."** Unlocks NG+.
- **Drops:** 500 coins, 30 gems, 10 map fragments, **FINAL SHIPWRIGHT CHEST** (guaranteed free evolution of any held L5 weapon, **bypasses pairing requirement** — "wild card" end-of-campaign gift), "Kraken Slayer" leaderboard title, "Kraken Crown" cosmetic (crown + tentacle wake trail).
- **Flavor:** tentacles emerge as far as eye can see; water roils; deep bass note intro. Defeat: cinematic sink + end credits + NG+ unlock.
