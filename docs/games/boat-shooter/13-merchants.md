# Merchants & Chests

## Shipwright Cove (main merchant)

### Mid-stage Cove

- Around 90 s of active play, a dockside hut drifts into view on the bank.
- Sail alongside for **15 s**; combat continues around you (enemies still spawn).
- Stock (refreshed per appearance):
  - 1 random **unowned weapon** — cost 15 / 30 / 60 by rarity (common / rare / gold).
  - 1 random **unowned passive** — cost 20 / 40 / 80.
  - 1 **stat boost** (+1 flat damage OR +5% fire rate OR +1 max HP) — 25 coins.
  - 1 **heal** (+3 HP, capped at max) — 40 coins.
  - **"Reroll Shop"** button — 15 coins (Admiral's Flag reduces).

### End-of-stage Cove

- After boss defeat, a full-screen Cove scene. No timer.
- All mid-stage items at **−25% cost**.
- 1 guaranteed **evolution slot** if pair is held.
- Spend **gems** for permanent meta upgrades (quick meta-shop access).
- Exits: "Next Stage" / "Campaign Menu".

### Shipwright Chest (boss drop)

- Dropped by mini-bosses + full bosses.
- Opens with a short cinematic + audio sting.
- If **L5 weapon + paired passive held** → free instant evolution.
- Otherwise spawns 2 random L-tier coins.
- **Final boss chest** is a **wild-card evolution** — bypasses pair requirement;
  player picks any held L5 weapon to evolve.

## Black Market Merchant (rare additional encounter)

- **10% chance per non-merchant stage transition** (never stacks with mid-stage Cove).
- Dark hooded figure, cursed lantern, ominous SFX on arrival.
- Inventory:
  - **Direct weapon evolutions** (skip pair requirement, pay gems):
    - Common-tier: 3 gems.
    - Rare: 8 gems.
    - Gold-tier: 15 gems.
  - **Cursed Relics** (permanent-for-run passive, risk/reward):
    - *Ghostly Compact* — -2 max HP, +50% damage.
    - *Bloody Sails* — -20% movement, +100% crit chance.
    - *Bone Ledger* — permanently halve coin income this run, gems ×3 instead.
  - **Forbidden Weapons** (exclusive, not in normal pool): reserved slot for post-launch content.

## Chests

### Tiers

| Tier | Open condition | Contents |
|------|----------------|----------|
| **Wooden** | Free open | 3–5 coins (M), 1 XP orb, 10% weak stat boost. Spawns from random barrels / destructibles. |
| **Silver** | Silver key OR mini-boss drop | 10–15 coins (M), 1–2 gems, 1 random weapon OR passive (any rarity). Elite enemies 5%, mini-bosses. |
| **Gold** | Gold key (boss drop) | 25–40 coins (L), 3–5 gems, 1 weapon OR passive (50% rare). Bosses guaranteed, elites 1%. |
| **Cursed** | Free (risk/reward) | **50% A:** +20 coins, 2 gems, 1 rare passive, 1 cursed relic. **50% B:** triggers spawn-wave ambush (5 Ghost Ships) *then* all of A. Spawns 1 per 3–4 stages. |

### Keys

- **Gold keys** — mini-bosses + bosses, guaranteed.
- **Silver keys** — elites 5% chance; Bank Sniper Towers guaranteed.
- Unopened chests persist across stage replays.

## Luck interactions

- Luck% chance per-chest that tier upgrades one step (Wooden → Silver, Silver → Gold).
- Luck meta L10 guarantees 1 Gold Chest per stage.
- "Lucky" variant of Cursed Chest (no ambush, just rewards) unlocks at Luck meta L4.
